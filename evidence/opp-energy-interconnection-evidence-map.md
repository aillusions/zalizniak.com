## PROCESS DYSFUNCTION / FERC ORDER 2023

| Claim | Tier | Source | Quote |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Over 10,000 active requests / 2,000+ GW at end 2022 | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "As of the end of 2022, there were over 10,000 active interconnection requests … representing over 2,000 gigawatts (GW) of potential generation and storage capacity" |
| 68% of interconnection studies completed in 2022 were issued late | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "of the 2,179 interconnection studies completed in 2022, 68% were issued late" |
| Shift from first-come serial study to first-ready, first-served cluster study | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "Transitioning from a First-Come, First-Served Serial Process to a First-Ready, First-Served Cluster Study Process" |
| Process is a 150-day cluster study → facilities study → IA, with restudies | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "the final rule includes a 150-day cluster study, followed by a facilities study before the interconnection customer enters into an interconnection agreement … the final rule provides for restudies" |
| Network upgrade costs allocated by "proportional impact method" | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "allocates network upgrade costs to interconnection customers within a cluster using a 'proportional impact method'" |
| New readiness bar: study deposits, 90%/100% site control, commercial-readiness deposit, withdrawal penalties | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "(1) increasing study deposit amounts, (2) requiring demonstration of site control, (3) requiring a commercial readiness deposit, and (4) imposing withdrawal penalties"; "90% site control at the time of submission … and 100% site control at the time of execution of the facilities study agreement" |
| Late affected-system network-upgrade costs cause late-stage withdrawals | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "leading to late-stage project withdrawals when affected system network upgrades are identified late in the process" |
| Providers now penalized for missing study deadlines (no more "reasonable efforts") | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "FERC eliminated the 'reasonable efforts' standard and introduced penalties if a transmission provider fails to meet study deadlines" |
| Providers must publish transmission-capacity heatmaps | VERIFIED | [ferc] | "requires transmission providers to maintain a publicly available visual representation, also known as a heatmap, of available transmission capacity" |
| Reform's effect not yet measurable | VERIFIED | [lbnl] | "FERC Order 2023 and various other reforms are being implemented … it is too early to measure and assess their full impact" |
