| Claim | Tier | Source | Quote |

| Three-step process: identify at dormancy → owner due diligence → report in NAUPA format | VERIFIED | [naupa-report] | "Identify unclaimed property … Attempt to contact the owner … Report property to the state … Most states require that the report follow the NAUPA standard format" |

## AUDIT / PENALTY EXPOSURE

| Claim | Tier | Source | Quote |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audits run by third-party contingent-fee/hourly auditors; usually ≥7 states piggyback | VERIFIED | [andersen], [bdo] | andersen: "Audits are generally done by third-party, contingent-fee, or hourly based agents who contract with participating states … most unclaimed property examinations have at least seven participating states" |
| Delaware audits: 15-year lookback, mandatory interest up to 50%, 3–7 years long | VERIFIED | [andersen] | "Delaware unclaimed property audits have a 15-year look-back period and assessments include a mandatory interest rate of up to 50% … it is common for unclaimed property audits to last three to seven years" |
| Reviews span 13–15 years with no meaningful statute of limitations | VERIFIED | [andersen] | "Most unclaimed property reviews span a period of 13 to 15 years, with no meaningful statutes of limitation" |
| Without complete records, states estimate liability — often higher than actual | VERIFIED | [andersen] | "states performing unclaimed property audits use gross estimation techniques to assess liability. It is not uncommon for holders to face higher estimated liabilities when compared to identifying the actual property" |
| Estimation has been judicially condemned (Temple-Inland) | VERIFIED | [decarrera] | DE's estimation a "game of gotcha" that "shocked the conscience" (Temple-Inland v. Cook, D. Del.) |
| Kelmar Associates is Delaware's dominant contract audit firm | VERIFIED | [taxadviser], [insidesalt] | taxadviser: "Delaware's contract audit firm, Kelmar Associates"; insidesalt: "Kelmar, one of the more notorious unclaimed property contract audit firms" |
| Delaware funnels holders via 90-day VDA letter → audit; SB 281 Verified Reports | VERIFIED | [andersen] | "Delaware routinely (quarterly) sends invitations … giving them 90 days to enroll … Any company that fails to enroll within 90 days is … subjected to an unclaimed property audit"; SB 281 "allows the State Escheator to review compliance returns for any reason" |
| California: 12% annual interest; AB 466 discovery via tax return; AB 2280 VCP | VERIFIED | [andersen] | "California has historically … enforced interest assessments (12% annually) … AB 466 … requires any company that files a California income tax return to answer … Has this business entity previously filed an unclaimed property Holder Remit Report" |
| Enforcement is escalating (CA VCP outreach; FL/IL/MA/UT/DC self-audit demands) | VERIFIED | [andersen] | "California sent out an initial mailing of about 4,000 letters late in 2025 … likely an indicator that the State plans to increase its … enforcement efforts"; self-audit requests up "in Florida, Illinois, Massachusetts, Utah, and Washington, D.C." |
