## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** County-record fragmentation with no APIs; chain-of-title abstraction by hand; judgment-heavy curative (liens, gaps, errors); underwriter review.
- **Why now:** Huge volume on a manual process; agents can now read varied recorded instruments and assemble the chain, flagging defects for human curative.
- **The money:** High volume, high-margin industry; per-transaction value; slow incumbent underwriters.
- **Whitespace:** Underserved. Record fragmentation and curative judgment kept it manual.
