Every company holding customer balances is legally required to report and remit unclaimed property to the states — most don't know it, and aggressive state audits carry large penalties.

**Vitals (seed — unverified):** market: large penalty + audit exposure across all firms · `per-state, annual` · buyer: corporate finance / compliance · model: subscription + audit-defense · whitespace: ★★★

## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Per-state dormancy rules and deadlines; mandatory owner due-diligence outreach; report formatting per state; audit defense with no organized evidence.
- **Why now:** So arcane most firms are silently non-compliant; states are escalating enforcement. Agents can encode the per-state rules and run the outreach + filing.
- **The money:** Penalty/audit avoidance is the wedge; recurring annual filings; every company with customers is a candidate.
- **Whitespace:** Near-empty. Extremely arcane, per-state rules, near-zero good software; awareness itself is the gap.
