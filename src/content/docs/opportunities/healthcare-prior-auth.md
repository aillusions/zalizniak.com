Prior-authorization packets bounce between provider and payer for weeks — a document-heavy, multi-party, regulated workflow that delays care and burns staff time.

**Vitals (seed — unverified):** market: large administrative healthcare spend · `high volume` · buyer: providers / clinics / RCM · model: per-auth or subscription · whitespace: ★★☆

## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Payer-specific requirements; clinical-document assembly; submission and resubmission cycles; status chasing.
- **Why now:** Agents-as-coworkers can assemble and pursue auths; durable workflow + document reasoning fit. Regulated and access-gated.
- **The money:** Huge admin cost; delayed care has real downstream cost.
- **Whitespace:** Some RCM/auth players exist; hard to enter without healthcare insider.
