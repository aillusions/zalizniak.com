Commercial properties are routinely over-assessed; appealing means deadline-bound comps research and evidence assembly that boutique consultants do by hand. The clean part: you can bill a contingency fee on the savings.

**Vitals (seed — unverified):** market: billions in over-assessment annually · `deadline-driven, per-jurisdiction` · buyer: CRE owners / consultants · model: contingency on savings · whitespace: ★★★

## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Per-jurisdiction deadlines and forms; comps and valuation research by hand; evidence packets assembled manually; hearings tracked on calendars.
- **Why now:** Provable ROI removes the budget fight; agents can now do the comps research, valuation reasoning, and jurisdiction-specific filing that consultants charge for.
- **The money:** Contingency on tax saved = zero budget objection, instant ROI proof. High-dollar per win, recurring annually.
- **Whitespace:** Near-empty for software. Run by boutique consultants on spreadsheets; jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction with no scaled tooling.
