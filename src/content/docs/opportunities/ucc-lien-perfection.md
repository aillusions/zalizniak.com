Lenders perfect security interests via UCC filings; a wrong debtor name, jurisdiction, or missed continuation can silently void priority and collateral. It's arcane Article 9 mechanics on hard deadlines.

**Vitals (seed — unverified):** market: high-value secured lending · `deadline-driven, recurring` · buyer: lenders / lessors / law firms · model: per-filing + monitoring subscription · whitespace: ★★★

## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Exact-name and jurisdiction rules that void filings if wrong; continuation/amendment deadlines; portfolio-wide monitoring; lapse alerts.
- **Why now:** Sleepy incumbent filing agents; agents can now verify debtor data, pick jurisdiction, and run continuous deadline monitoring across a portfolio.
- **The money:** Loss of priority = loss of collateral on high-value loans; recurring monitoring revenue.
- **Whitespace:** Near-empty for AI-native. Arcane rules, deadline-driven, sleepy filing-service incumbents.
