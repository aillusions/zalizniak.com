Every death triggers a months-long, multi-party paper ordeal: probate court filings, asset inventory, creditor notices, tax returns, and distribution to heirs — coordinated today by solo attorneys and executors on spreadsheets and fax.

**Vitals (seed — unverified):** market: generational wealth transfer in the tens of trillions · `~2–3M US estates/yr` · buyer: estate attorneys / fiduciaries / banks · model: per-estate or subscription · whitespace: ★★★

## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** County-by-county court forms with no standard and no API; asset discovery across dozens of institutions by mail; creditor/heir coordination over months; estate tax assembly.
- **Why now:** A once-in-history wealth-transfer wave is hitting an artisanal, paper-bound process; agents can now read varied institutional documents and drive long-running, multi-party workflows.
- **The money:** Huge non-cyclical volume; recession-proof; fees today flow to attorneys and corporate fiduciaries — an agent could compress weeks of paralegal time per estate.
- **Whitespace:** Near-empty. Fragmented by jurisdiction, emotionally loaded, no APIs; served by solo practitioners and spreadsheets, almost no AI-native players.
