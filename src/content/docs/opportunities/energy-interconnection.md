Renewable and storage projects stall for years in utility interconnection queues and local permitting — a multi-party, document- and deadline-heavy bottleneck on the decade's biggest infrastructure build-out.

## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Utility-specific application packets; AHJ permit forms by jurisdiction; multi-round study/revision cycles; deadline tracking across utility, AHJ, and developer.
- **Why now:** The build-out is exploding while the queue process is notoriously slow and manual; the pain is new, so no incumbent is entrenched.
- **The money:** Every delayed megawatt is lost revenue; developers will pay to de-risk and accelerate. High per-project value, growing volume.
- **Whitespace:** Near-empty and new. Multi-party, document + deadline; few tools, fast-growing demand.
