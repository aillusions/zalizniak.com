## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Identity verification; live witnessing/recording; per-state RON rules; tamper-evident audit.
- **Why now:** Adds ID verification + RON on top of signing; opens real estate, estate, and finance workflows.
- **The money:** Per-transaction; gateway to higher-value regulated verticals.
- **Whitespace:** Incumbents exist (Notarize, etc.); best as an upsell, not a standalone bet.
