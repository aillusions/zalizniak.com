Tens of millions of tax notices go out each year; taxpayers panic, overpay, or ignore them. Resolution is deadline-driven, document-heavy, and served by call-center firms rather than software.

**Vitals (seed — unverified):** market: tens of millions of notices/yr · `deadline-driven` · buyer: taxpayers / SMBs / preparers · model: contingency or subscription · whitespace: ★★☆

## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Notice triage and classification; correct response/form selection; payment-plan and abatement assembly; deadline tracking.
- **Why now:** Stigmatized and manual; agents can classify the notice, reason about options, and draft the response — work call-centers charge heavily for.
- **The money:** Overpayment avoidance + resolution fees; both contingency and subscription models work; vast volume.
- **Whitespace:** Served by call-center firms, not software; AI-native angle is open.
