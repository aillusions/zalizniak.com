The sources these notes are distilled from, plus the practice resources worth your time. The pages here are my own reorganized working reference — go to the originals for the full treatment.

## Distilled from

- [The best way to prepare for system design interviews](https://www.reddit.com/r/leetcode/comments/1el083l/the_best_way_to_prepare_for_system_design/) — the practice-loop method (struggle first, read the answer key last).
- [Hello Interview — Delivery Framework](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/in-a-hurry/delivery) — the in-room structure behind [The Delivery Framework](/system-design/delivery-framework/).

## Primers & foundations

- [System Design in a Hurry](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/in-a-hurry/introduction) (Hello Interview) — condensed, interview-focused: core concepts, key technologies, numbers to know.
- *System Design Interview – An Insider's Guide* (Alex Xu) — the standard primer; broad coverage of the building blocks.
- [Jordan Has No Life](https://www.youtube.com/@jordanhasnolife5163) (YouTube) — deep, practical walkthroughs.

## Practice resources

- Hello Interview — Common Problems: worked question breakdowns with answer keys, e.g. [Bitly](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/problem-breakdowns/bitly), [Ticketmaster](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/problem-breakdowns/ticketmaster), [WhatsApp](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design/problem-breakdowns/whatsapp).
- [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com/) — virtual whiteboard for timed practice.
