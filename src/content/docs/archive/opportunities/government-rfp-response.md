## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** RFP discovery across fragmented portals; compliance-matrix parsing; response drafting to strict format; deadline management.
- **Why now:** Existing tools are search-only; agents can now read compliance requirements and draft formatted, compliant responses, not just surface opportunities.
- **The money:** Each won contract = years of revenue; firms leave huge sums on the table by not bidding.
- **Whitespace:** Search incumbents exist; drafting/compliance-assembly is open.
