## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Copy-paste across legacy TMS/WMS/ERP portals; quoting and order entry by hand; reconciliation; per-customer quirks.
- **Why now:** Browser automation as fallback + durable workflows + per-tenant memory; the Pallet-style pattern, applied to a new vertical.
- **The money:** Replaces direct labor; high frequency; sticky once embedded.
- **Whitespace:** Whitespace depends on the chosen vertical; requires picking and learning one + browser automation.
