## Seed thesis

- **The mess:** Liability reasoning over messy claim documents; identifying recoverable files at scale; demand assembly; pursuit and tracking.
- **Why now:** Pure money recovery — ROI is the pitch; agents can now do the document forensics and liability reasoning at a scale humans can't.
- **The money:** Contingency on recovered dollars; carriers know the leakage exists but lack tooling.
- **Whitespace:** Thin competition. Document-forensics + liability reasoning at scale is underserved.
