## About Me

I'm Oleksandr. Twenty years on backend systems: cloud infrastructure and microservices on AWS, across SaaS, e-commerce, fintech, and telecom. Founding engineer and CTO of an AI native SaaS platform.

My passion is effortless durable execution, workflow engines, spec-driven development, software factories and formal methods.

I live in Upstate, NY.

## <span class="product-logo"><img class="logo-light" src="/logos/flowrensic.svg" alt="" /><img class="logo-dark" src="/logos/flowrensic-dark.svg" alt="" /></span>Flowrensic

[Flowrensic](https://flowrensic.com/) is forensics for durable execution. Your workflow engine gives you histories and raw metrics. Flowrensic turns them into answers: stuck runs, histories that keep growing, retries that never help, and the workflow type behind the bill. Each finding comes with the reason.

Temporal first, self-hosted or Temporal Cloud. Cadence and DBOS are in early proofs of concept.

[Website](https://flowrensic.com/) · [Flowrensic for Temporal](https://temporal.flowrensic.com/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/flowrensic/)

## <span class="product-logo"><img src="/logos/speccraft.png" alt="" /></span>SpecCraft

[SpecCraft](https://speccraft.io/) is my software factory. Many AI coding agents work on their own, each on its own cloud machine, and one person steers them. I write the requirements. The agents write the specs, the tests and the code. Checks, formal models among them, decide when a task is done, so I read decisions, not diffs.

The site is a short guide to AI coding in three steps: coding agents, spec-driven development, and the software factory. It also has a guide to formal methods, the tools that check what agents write.

[Website](https://speccraft.io/) · [Software Factory tour](https://youtu.be/c-nXRwhQ-xI)

## <span class="product-logo"><img src="/logos/signus.svg" alt="" /></span>Signus

[Signus](https://signus.ai/) is an AI native contract intelligence platform, built around Sigi, a legal agent that generates, signs, analyzes, and tracks agreements at scale. I co-founded it and built it from zero as CTO: backend, agent service, frontend, and infrastructure.

The agent runtime runs on Temporal, with durable workflows for chat, redline, risk analysis, and contract questions. That is where I learned how hard these workflows are to debug once they are live.

[Website](https://signus.ai/) · [App](https://app.signus.ai/)
