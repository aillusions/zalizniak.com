## SpecCraft <Badge text="building" variant="caution" />

[SpecCraft](https://speccraft.io/) is what I'm building next: tooling to **understand, verify, and test complex concurrent and durable-execution workflows** — the systems where the dangerous bugs live in event interleavings no test suite ever samples, and where no existing tool both *comprehends* the logic and *checks* it.

## Temporal & Durable Execution

[Temporal](https://temporal.io/) is the substrate SpecCraft targets. After ~1 year building on it, I'm convinced workflows-as-code is the right default for anything that has to survive crashes, retries, and long waits — but I hit its sharp edge firsthand: complex workflows with many interleaving handlers become genuinely hard to reason about, test, and trust. Closing that gap — going deep on Temporal and Go — is my current priority.
